What we actually do

Less talk. More deployment.

Real skills, real infra, real projects. Here's what a typical semester looks like for us.

🛠️

Hands-on Workshops

Every two weeks we break something in AWS — then fix it. EC2 instances, Lambda functions, S3 buckets. You learn by doing, not watching.

🏆

Hackathons

48-hour build sprints with AWS credits. Past winners built a serverless attendance system and an AI campus chatbot on Bedrock.

📜

Cert Prep Cohorts

Study groups for Cloud Practitioner, Solutions Architect, and Developer Associate. We share notes, quiz each other, and actually pass.

💡

Guest Talks

Engineers from startups and MNCs share war stories. Last semester: a DevOps lead from Razorpay on handling Black Friday traffic.

🚢

Ship-It Fridays

Informal sessions where members demo something they deployed that week. No slides allowed — show us the running URL or it didn't happen.

🤝

Open Source & Collab

We contribute to AWS CDK samples, Amplify libraries, and maintain our own club infra as a shared learning repo on GitHub.
